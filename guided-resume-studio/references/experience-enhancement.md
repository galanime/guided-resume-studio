# Experience enhancement and no-metric writing

The goal is to make a verified experience clearer, more specific and more valuable
to a recruiter. A missing metric is a field to investigate, not permission to make
the sentence vague or to invent a result.

## Evidence ladder

Inspect these layers in order and record which layer supports each clause:

1. **Outcome metric:** revenue, cost, time, volume, conversion, accuracy, ranking,
   pass rate or another directly verified measure.
2. **Reach and scope:** users, matters/cases, classes, patients, clients, documents,
   jurisdictions, stakeholders, team interface, time span or delivery cadence.
3. **Complexity and judgment:** ambiguity, legal/technical constraint, risk,
   prioritization, trade-off, exception handling or decision criteria.
4. **Method and artifact:** analysis, review, research, lesson design, contract,
   memo, prototype, system, campaign, dataset, protocol or other concrete deliverable.
5. **Quality and feedback:** test evidence, review outcome, acceptance, adoption,
   error prevention, named stakeholder feedback or an explicit limitation.

If layer 1 is unavailable, combine layers 2-5 into a precise contribution-and-proof
sentence. This is a valid strong bullet; do not treat it as a gap merely because it
has no percentage.

## Safe upgrade operations

Permitted when supported by the source fact or a user confirmation:

- Replace a task list with `对象/问题 + 关键动作 + 交付物/判断 + 证据`.
- Make the object explicit: what was reviewed, built, taught, analyzed, negotiated,
  designed, administered or delivered.
- Name the constraint or decision that explains why the work mattered.
- Convert an implicit artifact into a visible deliverable without changing its status
  (draft, prototype, internal, simulated, supervised or production).
- Replace weak process counts with stronger verified reach or quality evidence when both
  are available.
- Use bounded qualitative results such as“形成可复用流程”“支持团队决策”“完成交付并通过审阅”
  only when the corresponding fact is present.

Forbidden:

- Inventing percentages, money, user counts, case outcomes, rankings, clients, team size,
  seniority, ownership or legal responsibility.
- Turning exposure, assistance or observation into independent practice.
- Calling a draft, mock, classroom exercise, clinic placement or supervised matter a
  production, commercial or independent result.
- Adding a result clause only because a bullet “should” have one.

## Targeted follow-ups

Ask no more than three questions in one batch, and only for high-value missing fields:

- scope: who/what/how many/how long;
- decision: what changed, what risk was handled or what trade-off was made;
- proof: what artifact, review, acceptance, feedback or observable state followed.

If the user cannot supply an answer, draft with the strongest supported evidence layer,
mark the unresolved field in `change-map.md`, and keep the wording bounded.

## Cross-role bullet patterns

Use the selected role-family strategy for voice, then choose the matching pattern:

- **Legal:** matter/context -> issue or risk -> research/review/argument -> document,
  advice or procedural result. Never imply bar admission, representation or case win.
- **Finance/consulting:** business question -> analysis/model -> recommendation or
  control -> decision/use evidence. Never infer savings or investment performance.
- **Education/training:** learner/context -> instructional design or intervention ->
  observable learning/feedback -> scope. Never infer score gains without records.
- **Healthcare/public service:** population or service need -> protocol/coordination ->
  safety, access, completion or handoff evidence. Never imply clinical authority.
- **Design/content:** audience/problem -> concept and craft choices -> shipped asset,
  review or engagement evidence. Never infer commercial impact from publication alone.
- **Engineering/product/research:** follow the selected family strategy, but still use
  scope, artifact and quality evidence when metrics are absent.
